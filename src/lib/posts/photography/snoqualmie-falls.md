A quick Saturday trip to the falls.

There are two main vantage points from which to view the falls. The first has a pedestrian bridge from parking to the upper platform and the second is down a small road with a boardwalk to the lower platform that goes past an active hydroelectric plant.

At the lower view there were men going up the rapids in a small boat for what I assume is practicing rescue operations. I was too interested to remember to take a photo.

You can use the log pile to find where the lower platform is relative to the upper.
